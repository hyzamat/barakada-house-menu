Printing tips

  • Print shop: send the print-shop PDF and ask for 90 × 50 mm, double-sided, 350 gsm (matte or soft-touch looks best).
  • At home: print the A4 file double-sided at 100% / Actual size on thick card (250–300 gsm), then cut along the marks.
  • Scan the QR on one printed card with your phone before printing many.
